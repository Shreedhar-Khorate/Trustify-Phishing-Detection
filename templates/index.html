🎣

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cybercrime where attackers pose as legitimate institutions (like banks or Google) to trick you into revealing sensitive data such as passwords, credit card numbers, or personal information.

🚩

Red Flags to Spot

  • Urgency: "Act now or your account will be closed!"
  • Mismatched URLs: `paypal-secure-login.com` instead of `paypal.com`
  • Generic Greetings: "Dear Customer" instead of your name.
  • Suspicious Attachments: Unexpected invoices or zip files.
🛡️

How to Stay Safe

  • Verify the URL: Always check the domain name carefully.
  • Enable 2FA: Two-factor authentication adds a layer of security.
  • Don't Click: Hover over links to see the actual destination.
  • Use Trustify: Scan suspicious links before visiting.
